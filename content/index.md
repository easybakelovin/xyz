## A little bit about me

Hello digital traveler. My name is Oscar Mendez, a mechanical engineer by degree, but a curious wonderer of all engineering, tech, innovation, culture, sports, economics - you name it.

This site is my attempt to blog all the learnings from my curiosities and interests into a somewhat organize repository that I can look back on later and say, "Wow what a waste of time that was". I joke, but in all the spent time reading, building and most importantly, failing, I have **learned** and gained priceless experiences. What better goal in life than to spread my ignorance?

All the fields I described above are evolving at the speed of light and it is impossible to keep up, and I find that extremely exciting. Among the unorganized, unfiltered, and sometimes unfinished chamber of thoughts that have somehow been converted into this project, I hope you find something that piques your interest, as it did for me at one point, and you are inspired to also learn and share experiences of your journey.

## What I'm up to today
Currently I'm in the process of studying for and taking the **Mechanical Engineering Professional Engineer's Exam** [[intro]]. When I'm not doing that, I'm tinkering with electronics, designing things to 3D print, outside biking or running, or reading up on stocks. Excited to share more on all of this as this project evolves.
