# Artemis

Interactive Zig game running on WASM. Hit `Enter` or click the screen and then you'll see me standing there. If you hit `w` key I will do a wave animation. That animation took forever for me but I had the opportunity to draw 8bit graphics and get the raw game development experience with [raylib-zig](https://github.com/raylib-zig/raylib-zig). Still a WIP..
